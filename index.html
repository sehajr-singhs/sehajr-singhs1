I'm a control and dynamics engineer in New York and a junior at Queens High School for the Sciences. I grew up hearing about the power grid from my dad, a grid operating engineer, and I keep coming back to one question. How do you make machine intelligence that understands the machine it runs on?

What I'm building
The Index of Operations: every machine draws a shape.
A machine's sensors trace a curved surface in high-dimensional space. IOO compiles any machine into physical operators joined where they trade energy and force, then learns the shape they draw together. It keeps the low-level signals world models throw away, and it holds when the sensors are recalibrated (OPS-SAT AUROC 0.749 to 0.736, where raw features fall 0.919 to 0.487).

Learning Lyapunov Functions for Power System Transient Stability: Neural Verification and Retraining Accepted
Sehaj R. Singh, Wenqi Cui. Tackling Climate Change with ML, NeurIPS 2026 workshop. A learned stability function passes on 99.98% of sampled states, and 200,000 random samples find no violation, yet a directed search finds them 6.1% of the time. Using the verifier as the sampler, then training through the CROWN bound, certifies the region to ρ = 2.5 on all five seeds where counterexample repair stalls at 1.8, with three independent verifiers in agreement.

The Shapes a Machine Draws Manuscript
2026
Every pair of a machine's channels traces a shape as it runs. Nine descriptors of those shapes identify 7 real systems at 99.4% across 309 records, and keep 99.0% under re-instrumentation where marginal statistics fall to 80.3%. The direct ancestor of IOO.

Stability-constrained neural adaptive control
2026
A bounded RBF network inside a Lyapunov-derived MRAC update law with σ-modification and projection, tested on four MuJoCo environments including a UR5e arm. 2.06° joint error vs 98.98° for the unconstrained ablation on the hardest contact task.

Time-Series Operator
2026
Time as geometry instead of tokens: delay embedding, Koopman lifting and a learned vector field, pretrained across domains. 31 of 40 zero-shot wins against a per-series GRU, and it recovers the 11-year solar cycle from a held-out channel (128 months vs 132 known). It also reports its own negative result at matched compute.
Electric Adaptation and OPERATOR
2025–26
The manifesto that started it: motor controllers that keep learning as hardware ages, inside a Lyapunov-stable adaptive core. Its "operators" became the index in IOO. MIT THINK Scholars semifinalist (about 29 of 1,042).
